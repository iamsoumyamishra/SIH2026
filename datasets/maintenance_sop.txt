MAINTENANCE SOP - MACHINE MC-1042 SERIES
Version 2.1
Department: Maintenance Engineering
Classification: Internal

Section 4.2 - Bearing Vibration Limits
Bearing vibration must not exceed 4.5 mm/s (RMS) under normal operating load.
If measured vibration exceeds this threshold, the bearing is considered at
risk. Corrective maintenance must be scheduled and the machine de-rated until
re-inspection passes.

Section 4.3 - Coupling Alignment
Coupling alignment offset must remain within 0.5 mm. Misalignment beyond this
limit requires realignment before the equipment returns to service.

Section 4.4 - Belt Tension
Belt tension must be checked and maintained within the manufacturer's specified
range. A tension below the minimum requires retightening before operation.

Section 5.0 - Corrective Maintenance Triggers
The following conditions require corrective (unscheduled) maintenance:
- Vibration above limit
- Alignment offset out of tolerance
- Oil or coolant outside operating range
- Belt tension below minimum

Section 6.0 - Re-Inspection
After corrective maintenance, the machine must be re-inspected against all
failed checklist items before approval to return to service is granted. An
approval note must be generated, verified, and filed with the maintenance
record.
